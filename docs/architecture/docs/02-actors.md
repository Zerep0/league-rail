## Actors
The following are the main actors in the system:

### Federation operator

The federation operator or entity organization is responsible for publishing competitions and enforcing rules. That is to say,
he creates and publishes competitions, sets the rules, regulations, categories, dates and participation requirements. In another word,
the federation operator controls all cycles of the competitions.

### Club Administrator

The club administrator is responsible for enrolling teams, buying services and receiving settlements. He can
register teams in competitions, manages inscriptions, hires certain services and receives settlements or liquidations from the 
federation operator. The club administrator is the main point of contact between the federation operator and the teams.

### Athlete

The athlete is the sportsman or sportswoman that participates in the competitions. He/She maintains a profile and
proves eligibility. The athlete can register for competitions, manage his/her profile, and provide eligibility documents to the federation operator.

### Fan

The fan is the spectator that follows the competitions. He/She can buy tickets, merchandise and other services. The fan can also follow the competitions, 
buy tickets for matches, purchase merchandise and other services offered by the federation operator or clubs. He uses his ticket to access the venue and watch
the matches.

### Sponsor

The sponsor is the entity that provides financial support to the competitions, clubs, teams or events. The sponsor haves a measurable milestones
and can receive reports and analytics about the competitions. The sponsor can also provide prizes or rewards for the athletes or teams.

### Venue Operator

The venue operator is the entity that manages the physical location where the competitions take place. He manages capacity, validate access, seats, gates, 
ticket validation and access permissions.

### Provider 

The provider is a abstract entity that can provide services like payment processing, travels, merchandising, messages. 

### Finance operator

The finance operator is the entity that manages the financial transactions and settlements between the federation operator, clubs and the rest of the actor's system. He
checks the financial transactions, manages the settlements and reconciliations, and ensures that all parties receive their payments in a timely manner.

### Auditor

The auditor is the entity that verifies the compliance and integrity of the system. He checks the financial transactions, settlements, 
and reconciliations to ensure that they are accurate and comply with the rules and regulations set by the federation operator. The auditor can also provide reports and analytics to the federation operator, clubs, and other actors in the system.
